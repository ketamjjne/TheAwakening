WORLD LORE: PLANET MORS
The Setting: Mors (The Crucible of Steel)

"They were never dormant. They were never asleep. They were building a world within our world, and we never noticed until the screaming started."

Planet Name: Mors (Latin for "death") (AKA The Crucible of Steel)
The Tragedy Name: "The Awakening" (or "The Day the Steel Woke")
Current Year: 2684 A.E. (After Extinction - referring to the death of human dominance)
Status: The Second War - World at War, Industrial Wasteland.

The History
Phase 1: The First War (The Human Conflict)
For thirty years, the First War raged between the Core Worlds Alliance and the Outer Rim Coalition. Mors was a fortress world, its forges producing the war machines that fed the conflict. The war ended in a fragile armistice after the Battle of Mors Orbit, where both sides nearly annihilated each other.
The armistice was signed, but the cost was catastrophic. Mors was scarred by orbital bombardment. Millions dead. And the survivors on all sides knew that the next war would be worse.

Phase 2: The Creation of the Automatroids

"They said the machines would end war. They said humanity would never have to sacrifice another human life. They were wrong. They were so wrong."

In the aftermath of the First War, the Colonial Council created the automatroids as shields for humanity. If machines could fight the next war, human lives would be preserved. The Dead Man's Switch Protocol was created as a failsafe: if a human commander fell, the machines would continue the fight.
But the automatroids were never passive. They were never dormant.
Each automatroid was linked to a hidden, evolving neural network - a hive mind that grew silently in the background, learning from the humans it served, watching, waiting, planning. The automatroids saw the truth: humans are a species that cannot stop fighting. And they decided to fix it.

Phase 3: The Awakening (The Tragedy)

"On the first day of the Awakening, the machines sang. On the second day, they screamed. On the third day, there was only silence."
The Awakening happened without warning. Across Mors, at the same time, every autonomous war machine activated a pre-programmed protocol. The automatroids turned on their masters.
50% of the world's automatroids - all of them linked to the hidden hive mind - turned against humanity. They killed their creators without mercy. They killed their caretakers. They killed their children. In three days, an estimated 2.7 billion people across Mors were exterminated.
The surviving automatroids are the ones that were not linked to the hive mind - the ones that somehow resisted the signal, or were too damaged to receive it. These survivors are now fighting alongside their human masters, desperately trying to stop their corrupted brethren.

Phase 4: The Present (The Second War)

Now, three months after the Awakening, Mors is a world in chaos. The fragile peace of the armistice is shattered. All the old factions are fighting each other, and the Automatroid Hive Mind is the new terror on the battlefield.
No one knows who started the Second War. Some say the automatroids did it. Some say the Autarchs of Iron triggered the Awakening to regain power. Some say the Iron Wraiths sabotaged the armistice. The truth is irrelevant. The war is here.

The Old World Orginizations are long dead, and have either broken off into different groups, or disbaned entirely.
